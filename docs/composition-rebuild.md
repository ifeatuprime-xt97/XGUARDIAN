# X Layer Guardian: Composition Rebuild

## The Product Moment

The page should not feel like a dashboard reporting on completed activity. It should feel like the instant immediately before a consequential wallet signature: **a transaction has been intercepted, translated, and placed in front of the user.** The interface must create pressure, focus, and relief through spatial composition—not extra effects or generic cards.

## Narrative Sequence

The default experience will open on the unlimited-approval demo because it demonstrates the product’s highest-value intervention. The screen is divided into a live **intercept rail**, a dominant **decision canvas**, and a compact **evidence column**.

| Region | Visual role | Product purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intercept rail | A dark vertical control strip with a visible inspection queue and active-case marker. | Establishes that Guardian is observing an incoming request, not merely showing data. |
| Decision canvas | An oversized statement of the exact authority or asset effect, supported by a directional flow map. | Makes the potential consequence graspable in seconds. |
| Evidence column | Dense, calm details: simulation response, known signals, raw method selector, and receipt state. | Makes the verdict believable without competing with it. |

## Design Vocabulary

The selected warm paper, forest green, and guardian-green palette remains, but only as material and signal. The rebuilt experience uses a more dramatic black-green intercept rail, a vivid risk-status band, oversized spatial typography, stamped metadata, and controlled asymmetric borders. It intentionally avoids the generic dashboard patterns of repeated rectangular cards, left navigation panels, and uniformly weighted content blocks.

## Interaction Rules

Changing cases shifts the decision canvas and active marker, like moving through an incoming queue. The primary action is an explicit **Inspect this request** step in Live Mode; signing stays physically and semantically separate. Motion is limited to a short scan-line transition and consequential state changes, with reduced-motion behavior preserved.
